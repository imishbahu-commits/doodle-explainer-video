# Emotion-to-Motion & Color Mapping

*Source: [LottieFiles/motion-design-skill](https://github.com/LottieFiles/motion-design-skill)*

## Motion Character by Emotion
| Emotion | Motion Character | Path Shape | Easing | Duration |
|---------|-----------------|------------|--------|----------|
| Joy | Bouncy, arcs | Curved upward | ease-out-back | 200-400ms |
| Calm | Smooth, flowing | Gentle curves | sine ease-in-out | 500-1000ms |
| Urgency | Sharp, fast | Straight lines | ease-out | 100-200ms |
| Sadness | Slow, downward | Drooping curves | cubic ease-in-out | 600-1200ms |
| Surprise | Sudden, expanding | Radial outward | ease-out-expo | 150-300ms |
| Elegance | Slow, controlled | Long arcs | (0.4,0,0.2,1) | 400-700ms |
| Playfulness | Bouncy, irregular | Arcs, squiggly | ease-out-back | 200-350ms |
| Power | Bold, large | Straight, commanding | ease-out | 200-350ms |
| Mystery | Slow, revealing | Gradual, masked | sine ease-in-out | 600-1000ms |
| Tension | Tight, restrained | Small oscillation | ease-in | 300-500ms |

## Path as Visual Language
- **Angular** = tense, mechanical, precise
- **Curved** = friendly, natural, organic
- **Spiral** = whimsical, creative, playful
- **Diagonal** = purposeful, dynamic, progressive
- **Vertical** = growth, weight, authority
- **Horizontal** = progress, journey, stability
- **Circular** = continuous, complete, infinite
- **Zigzag** = chaotic, energetic, unstable

## Temperature + Saturation + Contrast = Mood
| Mood | Temperature | Saturation | Contrast |
|------|-------------|------------|----------|
| Happy | Warm (+15-30) | High (+10-20%) | Medium |
| Sad | Cool (-10-20) | Low (-15-25%) | Low |
| Tense | Cool (-15-30) | Low (-10-20%) | High |
| Romantic | Warm (+10-20) | Medium | Soft |
| Epic | Neutral to cool | High, selective | Very high |
| Nostalgic | Warm (+10) | Low (-15%) | Low-medium |
| Horror | Cool, green tint | Very low | Very high |
| Dream | Warm (+5-10) | Slight glow | Very low |
