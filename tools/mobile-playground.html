Qwen Media Generator

Generate images & videos on your phone with your TokensRouter key. Your key stays on THIS phone — it is never uploaded anywhere except TokensRouter.

⚠️ If this page shows a CORS / network error, your router blocks browser use — use the Termux app method instead (instructions in the chat).
Tip: after an image appears, long-press it → "Save image" to your gallery.
Videos: tap the video → ⋮ → Download (or screen-record).
