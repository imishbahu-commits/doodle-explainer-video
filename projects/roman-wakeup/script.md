## Narration (script + shot plan)

> Each paragraph = one chapter. Sentences drive hard cuts; `[plate]` marks the
> visual. Character plates are reused with pose swaps; world plates get a slow
> zoom-in on the second pass.

**Hook**
You open your eyes. You're in ancient Rome — and no, you're not the emperor. `[rome-street wide]`
You're one of a million people stacked inside apartment blocks so badly built, they collapse for fun. `[zoom rome-street upper floors]`
Welcome to the year one seventeen. `[character neutral]`

**Home**
Your home is an insula. Seven floors. `[insula-room wide]`
The rich live at the bottom. You live at the top — where the walls are thinnest and the rent is cheapest. `[zoom insula-room window]`
There's no kitchen. No toilet. One room. `[character shocked]`
And if the building burns, you're the last one to find out. `[hold character shocked]`

**Breakfast**
Breakfast is bread. Maybe some olives, if you're lucky. `[breakfast wide]`
No coffee — that hasn't been invented yet. `[zoom breakfast flies]`
You eat standing up, because chairs are for people with money. `[character neutral]`

**Work**
You're a porter at the grain market. All day, you haul sacks. The pay is one loaf a day. `[grain-market wide]`
But at least there's the public toilet — a long bench with holes. `[latrine wide]`
And the sponge on a stick. Shared. By everyone. Good luck. `[zoom latrine sponge]`

**Baths**
After work, the baths. You pay a quarter coin. `[baths wide]`
For that you get hot water, cold water, and gossip. `[zoom baths pool]`
The rich sweat in their own rooms. You get the big pool. Not bad. `[hold baths]`

**Games**
On holidays, the Colosseum opens. Free entry. Free bread. `[colosseum wide]`
You sit at the very top, a hundred meters from the sand. `[zoom colosseum arena]`
You can't see the fights. But you can feel the crowd. `[hold colosseum]`

**Health**
Got a fever? The doctor will balance your four humors — maybe with leeches. `[doctor wide]`
The water pipes are lead. `[zoom doctor pipe]`
The average Roman lives to about thirty-five. So enjoy the bread. `[character shocked]`

**Outro**
And yet — they built aqueducts that still stand, roads we still drive on, laws we still quote. `[rome-street wide, slow zoom-out feel via reverse]`
You woke up in the wrong century. `[character neutral]`
But not the wrong civilization. `[hold rome-street]`
