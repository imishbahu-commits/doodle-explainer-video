# Topic Brief — Bataspis crux

## Winning package

- **Title:** Scientists Found a 419-Million-Year-Old Fish With a Batwing Head
- **Thumbnail:** BATWING HEAD?
- **Niche:** Animated Fossil Mysteries / discovery-driven paleontology explainers
- **Subject:** *Bataspis crux*, a newly described Early Devonian galeaspid from Yunnan, China
- **Core curiosity:** Why would a jawless fish evolve a skull almost three times wider than it was long?
- **Mechanism payoff:** Computational fluid dynamics found that the headshield generated unusually efficient lift, achieving the highest lift-to-drag ratio among the galeaspids tested.
- **Bigger meaning:** Early vertebrates experimented with specialized locomotion before jaws and flexible paired fins became standard.
