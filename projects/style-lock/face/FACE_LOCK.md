# Face + body lock (atomic)

Source: user saw still + leaked-secrets video frames in git.
Use this pack after a wipe. Do **not** use lumpy potato heads.

## Face plate
- Perfect **white circle** (not oval-blob, not lumpy potato)
- Pure white fill, **no shading**, no blush
- Thick even **black outline**

## Hair
- Short brown / orange-brown spikes
- **Only on the crown/top**
- Sides of the circle stay white (slight recede)

## Eyes
- Two **solid black ovals**
- Angry = slightly squinted
- Scared = rounder
- Happy = smaller / squint

## Eyebrows
- Two **thin black strokes** close to the eyes
- Angry: inner ends down (V)
- Worried: inner ends up
- Happy: raised

## Mouth + teeth
- Frown: short downward curve, **no teeth**
- Nervous: tiny **wavy squiggle**, no teeth
- Smug grin: wide curve, a few **simple white rectangle teeth** in the black line
- Laugh: open black oval, small white rectangles along the top only
- Never realistic teeth, never gums

## Face signs
- Sweat: light **cyan-blue teardrops** on the white plate
- Shake: small **zigzag ticks** around the head
- No extra symbols unless the beat needs them

## Body
- **Thin black stick lines** only
- Round black blob hands
- No clothes, no joint volume, no shoes
