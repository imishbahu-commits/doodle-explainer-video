## Face structure
- Distorted white **paint blob**, slightly **wider than tall**
- Bumpy uneven outline — **not** a compass circle, **not** a smooth oval
- Pure white fill, thick uneven black outline

## Hair
- Short brown / orange-brown (`#8B5A2B`)
- Crown spikes only; sides of the head stay white

## Eyes
- Two solid black ovals, slightly different sizes
- Angry = squinted · scared = rounder · happy = squint

## Eyebrows
- Thin black strokes
- Angry: inner ends down (V) · worried: inner ends up · happy: raised

## Mouth + teeth
- Frown: short down curve, **no teeth**
- Nervous: wavy squiggle, no teeth
- Smug: wide grin + 3–4 **white rectangle** teeth
- Laugh: open black oval + small rectangles on top only
- Never realistic teeth / gums

## Signs
- Sweat: cyan teardrops `#7EC8E3`
- Shake: zigzag ticks around the head

## Body
- Thin black stick lines, round blob hands
- No clothes, no joint volume, no shoes
