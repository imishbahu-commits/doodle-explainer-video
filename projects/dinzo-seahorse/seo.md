## Description
Being a seahorse is the strangest deal in the ocean. Your father gets
pregnant — the only male pregnancy on Earth — and carries two thousand of
you in a pouch for weeks. Then he gives birth in a violent, hours-long
labor, and swims away without looking back. Fewer than half a percent of
you will ever grow up. You are the worst swimmer in the sea, you have no
stomach, and you must eat three thousand tiny shrimp every single day just
to stay alive.

But if you survive the drift, you find your one true partner — and the two
of you dance together every single morning for life.

This is the complete life of a seahorse, told from the inside. Second
person. You are the seahorse.
