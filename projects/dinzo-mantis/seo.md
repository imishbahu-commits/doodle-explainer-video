## Description
Being a praying mantis is one long, violent summer. You hatch packed into a
foam egg case with two hundred cannibal siblings. You grow by crawling out
of your own skin, seven times. You become a perfect killer — the only insect
that can turn its head all the way around to look you in the eye. And if you
were born male, your entire purpose in life is to walk up to a female who
is bigger than you, stronger than you, and might just eat you.

The twist? Even if she bites your head off, you keep going. Headless.

This is the complete life of a praying mantis, told from the inside. Second
person. You are the mantis.
